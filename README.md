## Selected projects

### [Amazon vs. Walmart — Retail Growth and Profitability](https://github.com/jg5202-debug/Amazon_vs_Walmart)

A comparison of Amazon North America and Walmart U.S. across six annual periods (2019–2024), combining a reusable data workflow with business analysis.

- Built a four-module Python and SQLite workflow with external configuration and input validation.
- Calculated sales growth, operating margins, and revenue indices; presented the analysis in three comparative charts and a research report.
- Documented differences in fiscal periods and business mix to keep comparisons in context.

**Tools:** Python · pandas · SQLite · Matplotlib
